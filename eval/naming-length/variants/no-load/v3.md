## Naming

Every `s.Test("name", ...)` — at every tree level — names a business action or outcome. Forbidden: identifier leak (`UpdateQuote calls...`), HTTP codes incl. `4xx/5xx` (`returns 409`), assertion phrasing (`err is not nil`, `has the requested name`), structure phrasing (`with empty slice`, `returns empty list`).

Flag matches; read the `Test()` body; propose 1–2 domain replacements. Risky words in domain-natural sense (`returns the equipment`, `no past loans`) are clean.
